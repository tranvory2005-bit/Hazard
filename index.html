08Estimated Insurance Cost Range

Illustrative annual premium ranges for a home valued around $350,000, based on flood zone, wind exposure tier, and local disaster frequency. Homeowners and flood are usually two separate bills, not one — a lender may require only one of them depending on the flood zone. Individual quotes commonly land outside this range: wind mitigation credits (roof shape, opening protection, roof-to-wall connections) can swing the windstorm share of a bill by 20–45%, the hurricane deductible you choose below moves it roughly another 10–20% between 2% and 5%, and specialized carriers underwriting well-mitigated homes often price below the regional bucket used here. These figures are a planning range, not a quote.

How to read this report. This is a screening tool for early diligence, not a substitute for a flood elevation certificate, a wind mitigation inspection, an environmental site assessment, or an insurance quote. Flood zone data comes from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer where available; wind exposure and cost ranges are estimates built from public disaster records and regional risk patterns; wildfire, earthquake, and tornado ratings come from FEMA's National Risk Index (county level); nearby EPA-regulated facilities and Superfund sites come from EPA's Facility Registry Service and EPA's own ArcGIS-hosted Superfund layer.
